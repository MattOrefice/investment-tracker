{# ── Page 8: Methodology ────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Methodology & Data Sources

Time-Weighted Return
Daily-linked chain-linked TWR (GIPS-compliant). For each day t: rt = (Vt − Vt−1 − CFt) / Vt−1. Cash flows assumed at beginning of day. Cumulative return is ∏(1 + rt) − 1.
Benchmarks
S&P 500: SPY total return (adj_close). Custom Blended: SAA target-weight basket of benchmark tickers (SPY, QUAL, IWD, IWM, EFA, EEM, IEF, TIP, 50% VNQ + 50% DBC, BIL), normalized to $1 on inception date.
Brinson-Fachler Attribution
Allocation effect = (wp − wb) × (rb − rb,total). Selection effect = wp × (rp − rb). Portfolio weights = beginning-of-period market value weights. Benchmark weights = SAA targets. Sum of effects reconciles to active return within 1 basis point.
Price Data
Daily adjusted-close prices from Yahoo Finance (via yfinance), cached in SQLite. Adjusted close reflects splits and dividends (total-return basis). Weekend and holiday gaps are forward-filled from the last available trading day.
CAPE (Shiller P/E)
From Robert Shiller’s data (econ.yale.edu/~shiller/data/ie_data.xls). Monthly observations; current value is the most recent month available. Formula for implied 10-year real return: r ≈ −0.070 × ln(CAPE/16) + 0.066, calibrated to long-run Shiller data.
Macro Data
Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED, St. Louis Fed). T10Y2Y = 10-Year minus 2-Year Treasury Constant Maturity. DFF = Federal Funds Effective Rate. BAMLH0A0HYM2 = ICE BofA US High Yield OAS (available May 2023+).
SAA & Sleeve Targets
Strategic Asset Allocation locked in Phase 1: Equity 72% / Income 15% / Real Assets 10% / Cash 3%. Ten sleeves with tolerance bands (±2–3%). Full rationale available in the SAA section of the application.
