{# ── Page 6: Methodology ────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

Methodology & Data Sources

Time-Weighted Return
Daily-linked chain-linked TWR (GIPS-compliant). For each day t: rt = (Vt − Vt−1 − CFt) / Vt−1. Cash flows assumed at beginning of day. Cumulative return is ∏(1 + rt) − 1.
Benchmarks
S&P 500: SPY total return (adj_close). Custom Blended: SAA target-weight basket of benchmark tickers (SPY, QUAL, IWD, IWM, EFA, EEM, IEF, TIP, 50% VNQ + 50% DBC, BIL), normalized to $1 on inception date.
Brinson-Fachler Attribution
Allocation effect = (wp − wb) × (rb − rb,total). Selection effect = wp × (rp − rb). Portfolio weights = beginning-of-period market value weights. Benchmark weights = SAA targets. Sum of effects reconciles to active return within 1 basis point.
Drift Status
A sleeve is flagged “Drift” when either threshold is breached: absolute drift exceeds 200 bps, or relative drift exceeds 20% of the target weight. Both thresholds must be satisfied for a “Within” status.
Style Box Methodology
Size (y-axis): log10(weighted-average market cap in $B), additive-shifted so SPY anchors at y=2.0 (Large/Blend centre). Large/Mid boundary ≈ $79B, Mid/Small boundary ≈ $7.9B at current calibration. Value-growth (x-axis): four-factor z-score — book-to-price, earnings-to-price, dividend yield, cash-flow-to-price — each normalised as fractional deviation from SPY, averaged, then mapped to x with VALUE_SCALE=0.85; SPY/VOO anchors at x=1.0 (Blend centre). Value tilt pulls x below 1.0; growth tilt pushes above. Simplification disclosure: this is a 4-factor trailing approximation of Morningstar’s proprietary 10-factor score (which also incorporates forward P/E, price-to-sales, and earnings-growth metrics with undisclosed weights). Treat dot positions as directional indicators of factor tilt, not precise Morningstar equivalents. Universe: US equity ETFs only (VOO, VTV, SPHQ, AVUV); non-US sleeves (VEA, IEMG) are listed in a separate callout. Data: static ETF metadata from fact sheets; refresh cadence stamped by the “as_of” field in data/etf_metadata.json.
Price Data
Daily adjusted-close prices from Yahoo Finance (via yfinance), cached in SQLite. Adjusted close reflects splits and dividends (total-return basis). Weekend and holiday gaps are forward-filled from the last available trading day.
CAPE (Shiller P/E)
From Robert Shiller’s data (econ.yale.edu/~shiller/data/ie_data.xls). Monthly observations; current value is the most recent month available. Formula for implied 10-year real return: r ≈ −0.070 × ln(CAPE/16) + 0.066, calibrated to long-run Shiller data.
Macro Data
Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED, St. Louis Fed). T10Y2Y = 10-Year minus 2-Year Treasury Constant Maturity. DFF = Federal Funds Effective Rate. BAMLH0A0HYM2 = ICE BofA US High Yield OAS (available May 2023+).
